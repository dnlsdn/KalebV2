# Screws and nuts still needed

Counted from michaelkubina's [assembly guide](https://github.com/michaelkubina/SpotMicroESP32/tree/master/assembly),
step by step, for everything after session 9. The legs, the shoulder joints and the outer shoulders
are done and are not counted here.

| Step | M3x8 | M3 nuts | Note |
|---|---|---|---|
| Body: inner shoulders and chassis | 24 | 24 | 8 hold the blocks to the inner shoulders, 16 the chassis |
| Mount legs to chassis | 8 | 8 | four per leg group |
| Head with ESP32-CAM | 6 | 4 | after it walks |
| Tail / rear cover | 4 | 4 | the guide's tail section is written around a deprecated TFT part |
| Joining the cover halves | 8 | 8 | top and bottom, four each |
| Mounting head, tail and covers | 16 | 16 | nuts go into the top and bottom shoulder blocks |
| **Total** | **66** | **64** | buy round numbers, a hundred of each |

**M3x20** are all used up in the legs and the shoulder joints; nothing left needs them.

**M2x8 and M2 nuts** are only needed if the servohorns on the outer shoulders are to carry the full
six screws each rather than the two the guide allows: that is 24 of each for the four horns. Every
other horn on the robot is already mounted.

**One M2 rule that has already cost time twice:** a nut goes on every M2x8 before it is screwed into
a horn. Without it the screw stands proud on the far side and jams the servo.

**Possibly M3x12.** On this build the M3x8 looked too short where the shoulder blocks meet the inner
shoulder. Measure from the outer face of the inner shoulder to the nut before buying: if it is more
than 6mm, that joint wants an M3x12 or M3x16. The reference build uses M3x8 there, so check the nut
is in the pocket that lines up with the hole before assuming the screw is wrong.
