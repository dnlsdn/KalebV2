Power wires are drawn in the colours on the bench. Dupont colours are a convention for this drawing: 5V red, 3.3V violet, SDA yellow, SCL orange, signals blue, analog green. Screw positions on the bus bars are illustrative. There is no relay: the servo rail is live whenever the pack is connected, and in the complete robot the main switch turns it on and off.
